#间断点 
$41.\text{求函数 }f(x)=\begin{cases}\frac{x\mid x+1\mid}{\ln\mid x\mid},&x\neq0,\\\\1,&x=0&\end{cases}\text{的间断点并指出类型}.$

$\lim_{x \to 0} f(x) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x|x+1|}{\ln|x|} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{\ln|x|} = 0$，x=0为可去间断点.
    
$\lim_{x \to 1} f(x) = \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x|x+1|}{\ln|x|} = \infty$，x=1为无穷间断点.
    
$\lim_{x \to -1} f(x) = \lim_{x \to -1} \frac{x|x+1|}{\ln|x|} = -\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{|x+1|}{\ln(-x)} = -\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{|x+1|}{\ln[1-(x+1)]}$
    
$= \lim_{x \to -1} \frac{|x+1|}{x+1} \to \begin{cases} -1, & x \to -1^- \\ 1, & x \to -1^+ \end{cases}$
    
$x=-1$为跳跃间断点.
